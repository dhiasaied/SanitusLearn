MODULE 2. INTERVENTION IN HYGIENE AND HEALTH CARE IN INSTITUTIONS.

Topic 2. Apply organizational techniques to material resources allocated to personal care, monitoring the provision of services to users

Learning Outcomes

  • Identify the resources and materials necessary for carrying out personal care activities, differentiating them according to their application.
  • Detect the need to replenish material resources, verifying their foreseeable depletion, considering health needs, among others.
  • In a practical scenario involving the execution of activities with users, assign the development of these activities to each professional according to their training/specialization, also recognizing their level of experience.
  • In a practical scenario involving the monitoring of specialized regimens and services for several users, verify that the established medical and personal prescriptions are followed, recording any incidents or errors.
  • Verify the results of cleaning, laundry, and linen services in relation to compliance with the requirements of the institutional center or residence, recording any incidents or errors.
  • Distinguish the hygienic and sanitary conditions that products intended for human consumption must meet, recognizing their state of use and preservation.
  • Adapt menus to the preferences and needs of a hypothetical person by requesting/collecting their comments and evaluations, as directed by a hypothetical responsible person.
