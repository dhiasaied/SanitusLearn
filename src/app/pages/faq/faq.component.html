Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Here you can find answers to the most common questions about the Training Campus.

You will receive an email with your username and a temporary password. When you log in for the first time, you will be asked to change your password and complete your personal profile.

Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page and follow the instructions. If you do not receive the reset email, check your spam folder or contact technical support.

Make sure you have a stable internet connection and try to use an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). If the problem persists, try clearing the browser cache or using another device.

The questionnaires and evaluations are completed directly from the campus. You must answer all the questions and then click on the "Submit" button. Some activities may allow several attempts, while others only one.

You can use the Contact page of the campus to send us a message, or write in the corresponding forum for doubts of your module. For technical problems, we recommend using the contact form and describing the problem in detail.
